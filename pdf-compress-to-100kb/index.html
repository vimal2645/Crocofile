Compress PDF to 100KB

Portals with strict 100KB limits reject most PDFs. This tool tries to reduce your file to the limit entirely in your browser.

Click or drop a PDF

Use this PDF compressor when a form, exam application, or online portal sets a strict 100KB file-size limit. Select a PDF and the page attempts to reduce its size by rebuilding pages as JPEG images, with processing performed in your browser. This can make scanned or image-heavy documents smaller, but the visual detail may be reduced and the final size can depend on the contents of the original file. Review the downloaded PDF to ensure text and images remain legible and that it meets the destination’s requirements. The tool is focused on a 100KB target rather than document editing. If a 100KB limit is not required, the general PDF compressor offers additional size targets.
