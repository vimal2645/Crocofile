Convert CSV, XLSX, or XLS files to PDF with page-break and layout controls. Files never leave your device. 100% private.

Click or drop a spreadsheet

Supports .csv, .xls, and .xlsx

Best for simple-to-moderate documents. Complex layouts, charts, and exotic fonts may not convert perfectly.

Convert a CSV, XLS, or XLSX spreadsheet into a PDF for printing, sharing, or archiving a fixed version of its information. Choose the page size and orientation, then set how the sheet should fit and where page breaks should fall. Options include repeating a header row, adding bookmarks, setting header or footer text, and applying a watermark or password. The conversion happens in your browser; the spreadsheet is not uploaded. These controls can help make a wide table easier to read on paper or keep related rows together across pages. Spreadsheet structures differ, so preview and inspect the downloaded PDF for page breaks, clipped columns, and legibility before distributing it. Your source spreadsheet is not changed.
