Compress PDF to 100KB

Portals with strict 100KB limits reject most PDFs. This tool tries to reduce your file to the limit entirely in your browser.

Click or drop a PDF

Why does my portal reject PDFs?

Government, visa, university, and job portals often enforce a hard 100KB upload limit. A single scanned page with an image can exceed that easily. Compressing here rebuilds each page as a JPEG, which drops the file size without needing a server.

Is my file uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser using JavaScript. Your PDF never leaves your device.
