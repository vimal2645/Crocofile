Split PDF

Split into single pages or extract custom ranges.

Click or drop a PDF

Split a PDF when you need separate files for different page ranges or want to save individual pages. This tool lets you choose either to create one output per page or enter custom ranges, such as pages 1–2 and 3–5. The PDF is processed in your browser, so its contents are not uploaded to a server. Splitting can help prepare a document for a submission limit, share only selected sections, or separate a scanned packet into smaller parts. Review the ranges before starting and open the downloaded files to confirm the pages are correct. This tool works with PDF documents and is focused on splitting; it does not merge the resulting files back together.
