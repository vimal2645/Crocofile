Supports .xls, .xlsx, and .csv

Best for simple-to-moderate documents. Complex layouts, charts, and exotic fonts may not convert perfectly.

Convert a spreadsheet to PDF when you want to share or print its information in a fixed-page document. This converter accepts XLS, XLSX, and CSV files and lets you set the page size, orientation, and fit mode before creating the output. You can also configure repeated header rows, bookmarks, password protection, watermarks, and header or footer text. Processing takes place in your browser, so the spreadsheet is not uploaded. These options help adapt a workbook or CSV export for review, distribution, or printing. Spreadsheet layouts vary, so check the generated PDF for clipped columns, page breaks, and readability before sharing. The downloaded PDF is a separate output; your source spreadsheet remains unchanged.
