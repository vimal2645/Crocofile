Compress PDF to Exact Size

Reduce your PDF to a target KB. Runs entirely in your browser.

Click or drop a PDF

Max 50MB

Use this PDF compressor when you need to meet a file-size limit without uploading your document to a website. Choose a target in kilobytes and the tool works in your browser to create a smaller PDF. Common targets include 100KB, 200KB, and 500KB, which are often requested for form, exam, and portal submissions. Compression can reduce image quality or fine detail as the file gets smaller, so open the downloaded document and confirm it remains readable before sharing it. This tool is intended for PDFs that need to fit a size requirement, not for editing their text or page order. Select a PDF, set your target, and download the result when processing finishes.
