Organize PDF

Delete unwanted pages and reorder the rest. Drag thumbnails to move.

Click or drop a PDF

Reorder or remove PDF pages when a document’s current sequence is not right or contains pages you do not want to keep. After opening a PDF, use its page thumbnails to drag pages into a new order and remove unwanted pages, then download the organized document. Processing is performed in your browser; the file is not uploaded. This is useful for rearranging scanned papers, preparing a clean copy for a submission, or removing blank and irrelevant pages before sharing. Check the thumbnail sequence carefully and review the downloaded PDF to make sure the pages appear as intended. The organizer works with PDF files and changes their page order or inclusion, rather than editing the text or images inside each page.
