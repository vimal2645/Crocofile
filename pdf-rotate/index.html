Rotate PDF

Click pages to select, then rotate.

Click or drop a PDF

Correct pages that appear sideways or upside down before you read, print, or share a PDF. Add a document to view its pages, select one or more pages, and rotate them using the available 90-degree controls. You can also select all pages when the entire document needs the same adjustment. Processing happens in your browser, and the source PDF is not uploaded. After rotating, download the updated PDF and check the page orientation in a viewer before sending it. This tool changes page rotation rather than the document’s text or content. It accepts PDF files and is useful for fixing scans, phone captures, or documents assembled with inconsistent page directions.
